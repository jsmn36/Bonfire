Bonfire Munnar: A Luxury Hill Retreat Wrapped in Green

Wake up to cool mist, birdsong and the soft rustle of tea gardens at Bonfire, a luxury homestay in the heart of Munnar. Surrounded by green hills and fresh mountain air, our property is made for guests who want comfort, privacy and a real escape into nature.

Stay together in our spacious family villa, with room for everyone to relax, share meals and make memories. Or choose a private room with its own balcony, where you can sip hot tea, listen to forest sounds and watch the clouds roll past the hills. There are no traffic horns here, only wind, birds and the calm of the mountains.

Inside, rooms are warm, stylish and comfortable, a cozy contrast to the cool green world outside. Evenings are for fireside conversations, starry skies and slow, quiet moments, the kind of stay you will want to repeat.

Perfect for: couples, honeymooners, families and friends who want a peaceful luxury stay in Munnar.

Around Bonfire

Nearby places & photo points.

Just minutes from our door, some of Munnar's most breathtaking spots await. Plan your days, then come back to the warmth of Bonfire.

Tourist Places

Eravikulam National Park

UNESCO-listed home of the endangered Nilgiri Tahr. Stunning high-altitude grasslands and rolling montane scenery.

Get Directions
Tourist Place≈ 13 km

Mattupetty Dam & Lake

Scenic shola reservoir with speed boat rides, Indo-Swiss dairy farm and stunning tea estate reflections on still water.

Get Directions
Tourist Place≈ 20 km

Kundala Lake

Tranquil high-altitude lake with pedal boating, apple orchards and lush green valley all around.

Get Directions
Tourist Place≈ 9 km

Attukal Waterfalls

A beautiful tiered waterfall hidden inside dense shola forest — perfect for a cool morning visit.

Get Directions
Tourist Place≈ 18 km

Chinnakanal Waterfalls

Also called Power House Falls — a dramatic waterfall that plunges into a rocky gorge flanked by thick forest.

Get Directions

Tea Museum

Walk through 100 years of Munnar's tea heritage — guided tour of vintage machinery and working factory floor.
